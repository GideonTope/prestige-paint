Who We Are

Divine Mercy Prestige Paint Depot is a trusted family-owned paint business based in Ota, Ogun State. We proudly serve customers across Ogun State with premium quality paints for homes, offices and commercial buildings.

Over the years, we have built a strong reputation for quality products, excellent customer service and reliable paint solutions trusted by builders, homeowners and contractors.
