All 108 shades from Prestige's three printed charts, in the order they are printed. Tap any colour to see it big, with its code and how much light it gives back to a room.

108 shades

A colour on a screen is never the colour on your wall — your phone brightness, the time of day and the light in your own room all change it. Use this to make a shortlist, then look at the printed chart in the shop. The brightness number runs from 0 (black) to 100 (white): the higher it is, the more light that shade gives back, and the bigger the room feels.
