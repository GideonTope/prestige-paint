06 Paint calculator

How much paint
do you need?

Answer three questions and it tells you how many buckets to buy. No measuring tape and no maths needed. It is a starting estimate, not a final number — for anything beyond a small touch-up, have your painter or contractor check the room before you buy.

Question 1

How big is the room?

Tap the room closest to yours. You can change the numbers afterwards if you know them.

Most rooms are about 3 metres (10 feet) high.

No tape rule? Walk the room heel to toe. One normal adult step is about 0.75 metres, so four steps is roughly 3 metres. Close is good enough — we round up.

Question 2

How many doors and windows?

We remove them from the wall, so you are not buying paint for a door.

Question 3

Which paint, and how many coats?

Not sure? Leave it on Emulsion and 2 coats — that is what most rooms take.
