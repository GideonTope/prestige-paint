How The Calculator Works

Simple steps behind the result

1
We calculate your area

Using your length, width and height to get the total surface area — then subtract space for doors and windows.

2
We apply coverage rate

Each litre covers roughly 10 sq/m per coat on standard walls. We multiply by your number of coats.

3
We recommend the right sizes

We round up to full gallons (4L) and drums (20L) so you never run out mid-project.
